About EduLearn

Our mission is to make quality education accessible to everyone

Who We Are

EduLearn is a free, open educational platform designed to help students explore courses, access study materials, watch video lectures, and test their understanding through interactive quizzes.

Built as a fully frontend project, EduLearn runs entirely in your browser — no sign-ups, no servers, no cost. Just learning.

Curated Courses

Handpicked content across Science, Math, History, CS, and more.

Video Lectures

Learn visually with embedded YouTube video lessons.

Interactive Quizzes

Test your knowledge with instant feedback on every answer.

Progress Tracking

Track scores locally — your data stays private on your device.
